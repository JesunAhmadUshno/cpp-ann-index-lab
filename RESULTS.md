## Method notes

- Neighbour selection in the graph index is the simple variant (keep the M closest candidates), not the diversity heuristic used by FAISS HNSW. This is a plausible cause of part of any recall gap and is stated here rather than hidden.
- IVF training is spherical k-means on the first 10,000 corpus vectors (frozen protocol); the remaining vectors are assigned to their nearest centroid at build time.
- IVF storage layout: posting lists hold vector ids and search gathers vectors from the shared corpus array. At nlist 128 with nprobe 16 and above, scans touch a large, scattered share of the 60 MB corpus and become random-access bound: their p99 exceeds brute force in both recorded runs. Storing vectors inside the lists (the FAISS layout) is the standard fix; it is future work, not part of this study, and the effect is reported here rather than engineered away after the fact.
- Tail-latency variance on this shared VM is large. The brute-force p99 repetitions in the run of record were 32.94, 4.10, 4.53, 4.56 and 7.80 ms (mean 10.79, std 12.47). H1's speed ratio is computed by the frozen rule from the reported means; read the verdict together with this variance. The recall figures and all p50 figures were stable across both recorded runs.
- Every cell ran the full corpus scale (58,999 vectors). No cell was reduced for memory or time.

## Run provenance

Two full runs of the identical frozen protocol were recorded. Attempt 1 (results/run_full_attempt1.json) ran while a multi-threaded NumPy validation process shared the VM during its opening cells, so it was superseded by a clean re-run (results/run_full.json), which is the run of record for every number above. Both raw files are kept in the repo. Recall and memory figures are identical across the two runs; the three verdicts are the same under both runs. Within the H1 evidence the runs differ in which nlist carries the pass: attempt 1 passes at both nlists, the run of record passes at nlist 512 only, because the nlist 128 tuned point (nprobe 8) drew repeated tail spikes in the clean run (repetition p99 values 29.81, 9.03, 3.71, 29.86, 9.99 ms).
